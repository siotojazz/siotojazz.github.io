Instant readout Fast frame-level values from the current playback moment.
Peak -- Current sample-peak at the output meter.
RMS -- Short-window loudness body from the analyser.
Crest -- Peak-to-body swing right now.
Centroid -- Current spectral brightness center.
Low Band -- Bass share of the current spectrum.

Rolling 8-second view Averaged and held metrics that move slower and are easier to read while listening.
Peak Hold -- Highest peak captured in the rolling window.
Avg RMS -- Mean loudness body over the last 8 seconds.
Avg Crest -- Smoothed transient contrast across the window.
Avg Centroid -- Average brightness center over the same span.
Clip Pressure -- Average clipped-sample share in the window.
Bass Tilt -- Low-minus-high energy tilt over 8 seconds.
RMS Swing -- Range of RMS motion inside the window.
Brightness Drift -- Centroid spread for slower tonal movement.
